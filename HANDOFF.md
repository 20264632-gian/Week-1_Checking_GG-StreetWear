# HANDOFF.md

## Project Title
STREETWEAR - Urban Clothing Landing Page

## User Problem
Young shoppers who follow streetwear culture find most online clothing stores cluttered, slow to browse, and visually generic. They struggle to quickly see new drops, compare items, and feel a connection to the brand's identity before buying.

## Target Audience
- Teens and young adults aged 15-28
- Mostly mobile users who discover brands through social media
- Fashion-conscious, value limited-edition drops, and expect a bold, fast, easy-to-scan interface

## Chosen Aesthetic Theme
Urban / Hypebeast. A dark interface with high-contrast white text and a neon lime accent, big condensed headings, sharp square edges, and minimal decoration. The dark background makes product photos stand out, and the neon accent guides attention to key actions like "Shop Now" and "New Drop".

## Interface Goals
1. Clear navigation: simple header menu so users reach Shop, New Drops, and About in one click.
2. Readability: strong contrast between text and background.
3. Consistency: all colors, fonts, and spacing come from CSS variables in :root.
4. Visual hierarchy: hero section first, then product grid, then footer.
5. Responsive design: layout adapts from phones to desktops.
6. Clear calls to action: neon buttons that stand out from the rest of the page.
